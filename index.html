fatto.

A ledger of the meals and the moments you share with people. Photographs are made with the camera inside the app, at the table, and kept here.

Why did my photographs turn black and white?

Because they are meant to. Every photograph holds its color for three days from the moment it was taken, then turns a warm black and white and stays that way. Nothing is lost when this happens. The original is untouched on the device, and the black and white is worked out fresh each time the photograph is shown.

Do they ever come back in color?

Yes, once a year. On a moment's own date, every photograph in it returns to color for that whole day, then settles back. A moment kept on 29 February returns on 28 February in years without one.

Why can I not add photographs from my library?

The camera in the app is the only way in. That is what keeps the ledger a record of nights that happened rather than a collection assembled later. There is no import, and there are no filters or edits beyond the two treatments the app applies itself.

Why can I no longer edit the labels?

Who, What, and Remember can be changed while a moment is still in color. When it turns, they lock with it.

Where are my photographs, and are they backed up?

On your device. Back up to iCloud is on by default and copies them into the private database of your own iCloud account, so a new phone restores the ledger with its numbering, labels, covers, and fade states intact. Settings shows a plain status line, including when a backup is waiting because iCloud is full or the phone is offline.

How do I get a photograph out?

Press and hold any photograph and choose Save to Photos. It saves as it looks at that moment, in its current treatment. There is also a switch in Settings that keeps every original in Photos in full color as you shoot, which is off unless you turn it on.

Can I delete something?

A single photograph or a whole moment, both behind a plain confirmation. Moment numbers are never reused.
